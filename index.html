About Me

Hello! My name is Sen. I am currently pursuing a major in Computer Science (CS). My biggest passion is making homemade treats for dogs. I find joy in creating healthy and tasty snacks for dogs, as I believe it helps improve their well-being and strengthens the bond between pets and their owners. When I'm not working on projects for school, you can usually find me experimenting with new dog treat recipes or learning more about canine nutrition.

Besides my love for dogs, I also enjoy exploring the world of technology, and I’m particularly interested in how software and AI can be applied in the pet industry to make life easier for both pets and their owners.

My goal is to combine my passion for technology and animals, and I hope to someday develop products that improve the lives of pets through innovative solutions.
